❓ Help Center

Find answers to the most common questions about Conectio.

🚀 Getting Started

Click "Join now" on the homepage or visit the registration page. Enter your first name, last name, username, email, and a password (8+ characters). Click "Agree & Join" and you'll be automatically logged in and redirected to your feed.
Go to the sign-in page and enter the email and password you used during registration. If you've forgotten your password, the password reset feature will be available soon.

👤 Your Profile

Go to your profile page and click the "Edit profile" button. You can update your first name, last name, headline, bio, and website. Click "Save changes" when you're done. Changes are reflected immediately across the platform.
On your profile page, click your avatar image to upload a new photo. You can crop the image before saving. To change your banner, click the banner area at the top of your profile card. Images must be under 5MB.

📰 Posts & Feed

On the home feed, click the "Start a post..." area at the top. A composer modal will open where you can write your content (up to 3,000 characters) and optionally attach an image. You can crop images before posting. Click "Post" to share it with your network.
Click the ⋯ (three dots) menu on any post and select "Save post". To view your saved posts, go to your profile and click the "Saved" tab. You can also access saved posts from the sidebar on the home feed. Click "Save post" again to unsave.
Yes. Click the ⋯ menu on your own post, then select "Delete post". You'll be asked to confirm before the post is permanently removed. You can only delete posts you authored.

🌐 Network & Connections

Visit a user's profile and click the "Follow" button. Their posts will then appear in your personalized feed. You can also follow users from the "People you may know" suggestions on the home feed or the Explore page. To unfollow, click the "Following" button on their profile.
Use the search bar in the navigation bar. You can search by name, username, or keywords. Results are organized into "People" and "Posts" tabs. You can also browse the Explore page to discover trending content and new people.
Click the "Comment" button below any post. A comment section will expand with an input field. Type your comment (up to 1,000 characters) and press Enter or click "Post" to submit. You can delete your own comments at any time.
