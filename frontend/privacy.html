🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025

📋 Introduction

Conectio ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Conectio platform. Please read this policy carefully.

By using Conectio, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our platform.

📊 Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account data: When you register, we collect your first name, last name, username, email address, and password (stored securely using industry-standard hashing).
  • Profile data: Information you choose to add to your profile, including your headline, bio, website URL, avatar image, and banner image.
  • Content data: Posts, comments, likes, and saved posts you create on the platform.

Information collected automatically:

  • Usage data: We may collect information about how you access and use the platform, including your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and timestamps.
  • Authentication tokens: We use JSON Web Tokens (JWT) stored in your browser's local storage to maintain your login session.

🎯 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your profile to other users
  • Show your posts, comments, and interactions
  • Provide a personalized feed based on who you follow
  • Enable search and discovery of users and content
  • Send important service-related communications
  • Maintain platform security and prevent abuse
  • Improve and optimize the platform experience

🍪 Cookies & Local Storage

Conectio uses browser local storage to store your authentication tokens and user preferences (such as theme selection). We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

Local storage data remains on your device and is only sent to our servers as part of authenticated API requests. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings, which will log you out of the platform.

🤝 Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Public profile: Your username, name, headline, bio, avatar, and posts are visible to other authenticated users.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose your information if required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Safety: We may share information to protect the rights, safety, or property of Conectio, our users, or the public.

🛡️ Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms (never stored in plain text)
  • API endpoints are protected with token-based authentication
  • File uploads are validated for type and size
  • Input data is sanitized to prevent injection attacks

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

⚖️ Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all personal data we hold about you through your profile page.
  • Update: Edit your profile information at any time through the Edit Profile feature.
  • Delete content: Remove your posts and comments at any time.
  • Data portability: Request a copy of your data by contacting us.
  • Account deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

📬 Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@conectio.com.
